# **App Name**: Shepfarmsycpro

## Core Features:

- Dashboard Overview: Display key metrics like total animals, purchase costs, medicine expenses, feed costs, labor costs, sales, pending dues, and total dues.
- Livestock Purchase Tracker: Log the purchase of animals, recording details such as village name, farmer name, animal count, purchase price, and amount paid.
- Medicine Expense Recorder: Track medicine costs, including medicine shop name, date, cost of medicines, total amount spent, and any outstanding dues.
- Feed Cost Management: Record expenses related to animal feed, detailing costs for TMR, silage, groundnut, and other feed types to calculate total feed expenditure.
- Labor Cost Tracker: Document labor expenses, including dates, daily and monthly wages, number of laborers, advance payments, food and fuel costs, and total labor costs.
- Sales Transaction Recorder: Log animal sales transactions, noting the date, buyer's name, village, animal weight, sale price, any outstanding dues, and total amount received.
- AI-Powered Cost Optimization: Using entered cost data, analyze costs using the AI tool to give insight to the user.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Earthy green (#8FBC8F) to evoke a sense of nature and growth.
- Background color: Soft beige (#F5F5DC), a lightly desaturated shade of green to create a calm, neutral backdrop.
- Accent color: Warm yellow (#FFC857), analogous to green, will be used for call-to-action buttons and important highlights to grab the user's attention without clashing.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' for a clean and modern look that is easy to read.
- Use clear, representative icons for navigation, such as a graph for the dashboard and a cart for purchases.
- A clean, grid-based layout with clear separation of data entry fields and informational panels will enhance usability.
- Subtle transitions when navigating between sections, and visual feedback when saving or updating data will provide a smooth user experience.
